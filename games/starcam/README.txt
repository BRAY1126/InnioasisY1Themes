StarCam: a stargazing camera game
=================================

How to play: unzip this folder and open index.html in any web browser.
Nothing to install, and it works offline.

Goal: aim the camera at the night sky, find all 14 targets (9 constellations,
the Moon, Jupiter, Saturn, a comet and a shooting star) and photograph them.
Each photo is rated 1-3 stars based on how well you frame the target.

Controls
  Aim ............ Arrow keys / WASD, or drag the sky (top screen)
  Zoom ........... Q / E, mouse wheel, or the zoom slider and buttons
  Take photo ..... Space / Enter, or the SNAP button
  Change target .. [ and ], or the arrows at the top of the bottom screen
  Guide lines .... G
  Photo album .... P

Your star log and the last 12 photos are saved in the browser.
