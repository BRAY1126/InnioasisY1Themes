GROOVY GROW - a psychedelic farm clicker
========================================

WHAT'S IN THE ZIP
  groovygrow.nds   - the game (Nintendo DS / DS Lite / DSi / 3DS)

PUT IT ON YOUR R4 CARD
  1. Put the R4's microSD card in your computer.
  2. Copy groovygrow.nds anywhere on it (the root is fine).
  3. Put the microSD back in the R4, R4 in the DS Lite or 3DS XL.
  4. Pick "groovygrow" from the R4 menu.
  Progress autosaves every 30 seconds (and when you press SELECT)
  to groovygrow.sav on the same microSD card.
  Your farm keeps growing while it's off (up to 8 hours' worth).

CONTROLS
  Tap the big green button or press A ... pick a bud
  Tap a shop row ......................... buy it
  Up / Down, then X or R ................. choose and buy with buttons
  SELECT ................................. save now
  START .................................. boss screen (fake spreadsheet)
                                           press any button to go back

NO R4? (3DS browser version)
  Open the 3DS Internet Browser and go to:
  https://themes.innioasis.app/clicker/
